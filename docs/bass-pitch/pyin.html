The idea in one row

pYIN is classic YIN plus two upgrades: the single hard threshold becomes a probability distribution over thresholds, and the per-frame decision becomes an HMM path decoded over the whole signal.

1 · CMND per frameSelf-mismatch d′(τ) for every candidate period τ. Deep trough = strong periodicity at that period. Normalized: loudness-independent, ≈1 when aperiodic.
2 · Thresholds → probabilitiesIntegrate over 100 Beta(2,18) thresholds. Trough depth becomes probability mass per pitch candidate; the total is the frame's voiced probability — the calibrated "confidence".
3 · Viterbi over (pitch × voicing)898 states, ±5 semitones/frame pitch inertia, 0.01 voicing-switch cost. The path, not any single frame, decides f0 and voicing.

Glossary

Period / lag (τ)
A shift in samples. If the waveform repeats every τ samples, its frequency is sr / τ — τ = 621 at 22 050 Hz ≈ 35.5 Hz ≈ D1.
Autocorrelation
Similarity of a signal to a shifted copy of itself, per shift; peaks at the period and its multiples. FFTs compute all shifts at once.
CMND (cumulative mean normalized difference)
The mismatch energy d′(τ) between the signal and its τ-shifted copy, normalized by the running average over smaller lags. It hovers near 1 where the frame is aperiodic and dips toward 0 at the true period, making trough depth a loudness-independent periodicity score: lower = more periodic.
Trough
A local minimum of the CMND curve — one period candidate.
Voiced / unvoiced
Speech-processing vocabulary for "this frame contains a periodic, pitched sound" versus not. Here: a bass note is sounding in that 11.6 ms frame.
Beta(2, 18) / Boltzmann
Beta(2, 18): a distribution on [0, 1] with nearly all mass around 0.03–0.15, standing in for "the range of thresholds a reasonable person might pick". Boltzmann: exponentially decaying weights e−λk over ranks, softly preferring the first trough.
HMM (hidden Markov model)
A sequence of hidden states you never observe (the true pitch bin and voicing per frame) emitting noisy observations you do (each frame's candidate probabilities). "Markov" = the next state depends only on the current one, which is what the transition matrix encodes. Decoding asks: which hidden sequence best explains all frames at once?
Viterbi
The classic dynamic-programming decode — literally a 2D table over frames × states: best[t][s] = obs[t][s] × max over s′ of (best[t−1][s′] × transition[s′→s]), filled left to right (197 × 898 for bar 11), with backpointers so the optimal full path is read out backwards at the end. Cost ≈ frames × states × transition-band width, which is why it dominates the pipeline's runtime.
