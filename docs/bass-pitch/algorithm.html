Grid-Guided Bass Transcription

Algorithmic architecture of crates/bass-pitch — companion to docs/bass-pitch/algorithm.md

Core idea

Instead of free-form transcription, exploit what the project already knows — tempo, grid origin, track offset — plus one domain assumption: a bass stem is monophonic. Every note then starts and ends on a grid cell boundary, and transcription reduces to three small, ordered, independent decisions:

1Presence

Which cells have bass at all? Decided by loudness only. Tuned for recall: audible notes must never be dropped.

2Segmentation

Where is a new note articulated? Decided by onset novelty only, so repeated same-pitch notes still split.

3Pitch

What is each region? Decided by weighted pYIN votes. Pitch confidence weighs the vote but never gates existence.

The founding failure this design avoids: using pYIN confidence as a presence gate silently dropped most of an audibly playing bassline. Each decision also emits its own diagnostic output (staged MIDIs, per-frame/cell/boundary CSV records), so errors are attributable to exactly one stage.

presence / loudness segmentation / onset pitch / pYIN inactive / rest

Pipeline

Stage ideas

pYIN pitch tracking

Per frame, YIN autocorrelation yields a probability distribution over pitch candidates instead of one guess; a Viterbi decode over (pitch × voicing) states with a small-step transition prior picks the most likely path. Temporal continuity is what resolves the octave ambiguity every individual bass frame has.

Confidence is never a gate. Vote weight is 0.1 + 0.9 × probability — the 0.1 floor means an uncertain pYIN can weaken a pitch vote but can never erase a note that presence already established.

Onset novelty

Rectified frame-to-frame rise of log band energy, averaged over 128 mel bands and normalized by the excerpt's 95th percentile, so the 0.4 split threshold is relative to the material.

Two load-bearing details found by fixture failures: band-aggregate before rectifying (per-bin decay jitter otherwise rectifies into fake onsets), and delay the envelope by half a window (else every peak lands one cell early).

Activity (presence)

A cell is active when its median RMS clears a dBFS threshold, with on/off hysteresis. Runs of active cells form regions.

Deliberately recall-first and deliberately dumb: decay tails stay "active" (over-sustain) rather than risking dropped notes. Sustain-versus-decay classification is a planned refinement, not a bug being ignored.

Segmentation

A new note starts at every active cell whose peak onset reaches the threshold; other active cells extend the current note.

This is what pitch-change detection cannot do: four repeated D1 sixteenths have no pitch change, but each articulation has a flux peak.

Region pitch vote

Every voiced frame in a region votes its rounded MIDI pitch, weighted by confidence; the heaviest pitch labels the region. Regions with no voiced evidence keep a fallback pitch instead of disappearing, and runner-up weight and margin are recorded for diagnosis.

Chunked orchestration

pYIN dominates runtime, so ~10 s frame-aligned chunks with 32 discarded context frames per side run through the unmodified pYIN core (demucs-style core/orchestration split), enabling per-chunk progress and future parallelism.

Viterbi is formally global, but competing paths merge within tens of frames: on the full stem, chunked vs unchunked differs in 1 frame record of 14 022 and 0 decisions.
